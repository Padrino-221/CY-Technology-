Getting started

Sign in with your front desk username and password. The Dashboard opens after sign-in. Use the sidebar to switch pages and the account menu in the upper right for settings, this manual, password changes, and sign-out.

  1. Register customer
  2. Add laptop
  3. Create sale
  4. Record repayment
  5. Review reports

A sale connects one customer and one laptop. Repayments attach to that sale. Those same records feed the customer’s balance, the follow-up queue, and your sales and profit reports.

Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes outstanding credit, the number of agreements, laptops available for sale, and accounts that need follow-up. Select a card to open its underlying list. The in-stock card opens inventory with the stock filter applied.

Credit activity shows agreements recorded over the last seven days. Recent sales and the follow-up queue link directly to agreements. Portfolio progress shows the share paid in full. Sales and collections summarize cash value, deposits, repayments, and agreed interest. The profit section summarizes agreed profit, cash profit, and cost recovery.

Open Dashboard

Customers

  1. Open Customers and select New customer. Enter a unique customer code, full name, and phone number.
  2. Use Continue to move through personal details, education and repayment, guarantor and next of kin, then consent and photo. Back keeps entries as you move between steps.
  3. If you enter a Ghana Card PIN, use the printed format, such as GHA-123456789-0. The app checks the format.
  4. Choose a photo if needed and select Save customer on the last step.

Select a customer’s name to see their profile, photo, contact information, and credit agreements. The outstanding balance combines that customer’s agreements. Select Edit info to update details or replace or remove the photo. A customer linked to a sale is protected from deletion.

Open Customers

Laptop inventory

Select New laptop and enter the laptop code, brand, model, unique serial number, purchase cost, other costs, and selling price. Add specifications, a description, and product photos as available. Other costs can include transport or repairs. Total cost combines purchase cost and other costs.

To add several units of the same model, set Quantity on the new laptop form. Enter the first serial number in Serial and the rest under Additional serial numbers, one per line. The app adds numbered suffixes to the code for additional units and tracks each unit separately. You can also add units from Edit laptop. Quantity includes the laptop you are editing, so set it to 3 to add two more units.

Use All laptops or In stock to switch views. Open a laptop to see its product page. Select a thumbnail to change the main image. Use Edit laptop to change details, add up to eight photos, or mark photos for removal and save.

Saving a credit sale marks the laptop sold and removes it from the available options for new sales. The agreement stores its selling price and cost at the time of the sale. A laptop linked to a sale is protected from deletion.

Open Laptop inventory

Credit sales

  1. Select New sale and enter a unique sale code. Choose the customer and an available laptop.
  2. Enter the sale date, deposit percentage, term in days, and repayment frequency. Record whether the agreement is signed and add an officer or notes as needed.
  3. Save the sale and open its detail page. Check the deposit, interest, scheduled instalment, amount to repay, and due dates.

Interest is calculated on the original cash price. The total to repay after deposit is cash price plus interest minus deposit. For example, GH₵4,000 with a 70% deposit and 5% interest leaves GH₵1,400 to repay. Use the rates and limits shown on the sale form for the actual agreement.

The detail page connects the agreement terms, repayments, outstanding balance, and profit. Select Edit sale for corrections. Once repayments exist, the financial terms are locked. A sale with repayments is protected from deletion.

Return a laptop and track the refund

  1. Open the laptop or its agreement and choose Record return after receiving the unit.
  2. Enter a reason and select Ready for resale or Needs inspection. After inspection, open the laptop and choose Mark ready for resale.
  3. Enter the agreed refund if one is due. Saving closes the balance and preserves the original agreement and repayments. Click a repayment to read its details.
  4. After sending a refund, open the returned agreement and select Record refund payment. Enter the amount and payment reference or receipt number. You can record partial payments until the refund is complete.

The dashboard shows returned sales collections and refunds separately from the rest of the portfolio.

Open Credit sales

Repayments

Open a sale and select Record repayment, or choose New payment in Repayments and select the agreement. Enter a unique payment code, the date, amount, and method. Time, reference, recipient, and notes can help you identify the payment later.

Use a payment date from the sale date through today. The amount must fit the agreement’s remaining balance. Open a payment from the repayment list or from its agreement to correct it. Editing or deleting a payment recalculates the agreement balance, follow-up status, and cash profit. Full repayment closes the outstanding balance.

Open Repayments

Follow-up

The list shows accounts that are due soon, behind schedule, overdue, or in recovery. Use customer and guarantor contact information to follow up. Open the sale to see its next due date, final due date, and payments. Open the customer name to check their full profile.

Due soon
The next due date is within the warning period.
Behind schedule
The next instalment date has passed while the final due date has yet to pass.
Overdue
The final due date has passed.
Recovery
The final due date and the recovery grace period have both passed.

Select Print view to see all matching records, then select Print follow-up list to open your browser’s print options.

Open Follow-up

Cost and profit

Agreed profit
Agreed credit price minus the laptop cost recorded on the sale.
Cash collected
The deposit plus repayments recorded so far.
Cash profit
Cash collected minus the recorded laptop cost. A negative figure shows how much of the cost is still unrecovered.
Cost recovery
Recovered once the deposit and repayments cover the recorded laptop cost.

Open a sale code to reconcile the figures with the agreement and its payment history.

Open Cost and profit

Reports

  • Customer balances groups outstanding credit by customer. Select a name to review the individual agreements.
  • Accounts by status shows the count and outstanding balance for each status.
  • Sales by institution groups agreements by the institution recorded on each customer profile and shows the cash value sold and outstanding credit.

These reports update as agreements and payments change. Use the links at the top of Reports to open the follow-up list or see profit by sale.

Open Reports

Search and lists

Use the header search to look across customers, inventory, sales, and payments. Try a name, record code, phone number, serial number, or payment reference. Results are grouped by record type. Select View all to open that group’s filtered list.

Search within a list to narrow that page. Clear search resets the search text. Long lists show 25 records per page. Use Previous and Next to move between pages while keeping the active search and filters.

Settings and account

Open Business settings from the account menu to edit the minimum deposit, maximum term, interest rates for the three term bands, and follow-up timing. New agreements use the saved rates. Existing agreements keep their agreed interest unless the laptop or term is changed before repayments begin. Follow-up timing applies to all open agreements.

Use Change password to enter your current password and confirm a new password of at least 12 characters. You stay signed in after saving. Use Sign out when leaving the front desk.

Open Business settings · Change password

Troubleshooting

A laptop is missing from the sale form
Check the inventory status. Only available laptops can be selected for a new sale.
A payment will not save
Check its code, the payment date, and the remaining amount on the agreement.
A record cannot be deleted
Check the agreements or repayments linked to it. Edit the original record if you are correcting a detail.
The Windows app does not open
Run Start.cmd and keep its window open while using the app. Use the address printed in that window.
You need a backup
On the Windows installation, run Backup.cmd. See CLIENT-SETUP.md in the installation folder for backup and restore instructions.

Understanding profit

Profit after all debts are settled shows agreed selling value, including interest, minus costs for sold laptops. Profit from collections so far uses deposits and repayments already received minus those same costs. The difference is the customer debt still outstanding. These figures exclude returned agreements and general business expenses.
